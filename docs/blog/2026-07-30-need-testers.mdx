WTF 4.3 needs testers! Maybe that's you.

{/* truncate */}

Hello frens! We haven't seen each other in quite a while. Please don't look around on this website, most of it is very much under construction still.

WTF 4.3 is ready to be released, so it's time for the traditional test round. You'll find your copy of WTF 4.3rc1 in [Releases](https://github.com/Igigog/Weird_Task_Framework/releases) section of Github.

4.3 comes with a shitton of improvements. You'll get better and more balanced rewards, better descriptions, new quests, and obviously an infinite amount of improvements in the backend for the quest makers.

### Quests

1. GhenTuong's _Neutralization_ and _Eliminate dangerous mutants_ got a rebalance - they now require you to go to the location regardless of whether the squad got killed offline or not. You just need to confirm it with your eyes to make sure the quest is actually done. This rework replaces MCM option _Arszi's Realistic Assassinations_.
2. Community's _Between Law And Profit_ got a full rework - this is the first branching quest in WTF, where the way it plays depend on how long did it took to get to it.
3. New quests for Ecologists, _Onboarding_ and _Big Measuring Contest_. Both of them play around with an anomaly field measuring mechanic, and both of them will improve your artifact detector on completion.

You can find task descriptions [here](/tasks/gt-tasks).

### General improvements

1. Rewards are brought in-line with vanilla Anomaly balance. Well, as good as I could. I removed WTF-wide Reward sliders from MCM, but you can still configure them per task
2. Location descriptions in RUS localization now show the level of location, just like it's done in ENG.
3. Fixed a bug where GT's _Eliminate dangerous mutants_ didn't finish after killing all the mutants
4. _Utjan's Fetch Thing_ MCM option is now removed. The functionality will continue to work, I just don't see a reason to disable it.

### Backend

I can't even start to describe the work made in the backend. WTF 4.3 introduces multi-stage quests and branching, better reward and action handling, better descriptions and so, so much more. Aside from fancier, more fleshed out quests, normal players shouldn't see any difference, to be honest. If you plan to use WTF for quest making, at least wait until I wrote the docs.

### What now?

Here's what I'm looking for, specifically:
1. Bugs and problems
2. General improvement suggestions, including balance, game experience and whatever
3. Does it still work with PDA Taskboard?
4. Does it work on GAMMA?
5. Just tell me your thoughts

You can find me on Anomaly and GAMMA discord. I would prefer you to write your thoughts in WTF's forum post in Anomaly discord, but I'll also take normal pings.

Take care and good hunting, cuties.
