# Beacon of Science

<TaskView taskPack="community-task-pack" task="Community/tracking_shot.json" substitutions={{}}/>

<br/>

For a long time, I said that I only have made one single quest. This is that quest.

Beacon of Science is a product of collaboration of the whole community. The idea came from the legendary Nekrokant - the docs with his quest ideas was my bible for quite a bit of time. The blowgun and dart came from Mich, the creator of Boomsticks & Sharpsticks. The texts were written by the WTF discord community. The code, as it happens, was written by me.

Beacon of Science is a pacifistic mission where you mark a flesh with a special dart. You get two tries, after which... I don't know if I have a failure condition, lol. After that, you reload last save. It's somewhat hard to miss anyway.
