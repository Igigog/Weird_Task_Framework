Supply chain and it's brother, [Transaction](/tasks/gt-tasks/transaction), are the heavy-hitters of GT Pack. The premise is simple - there are two very heavily armed squads of enemies and a few packs with goodies at a position; go there, steal the goodies, do whatever you want with enemies.

I don't know if it's used much, but you can open the packs. This will fail the mission, but you get to keep the goodies; in Supply chain, these are bundles of ammo or medicine.
