<br/>

Equip task is probably the less balanced one of GT Pack. It will find a stalker in a location up to 2 map transitions from you with a gun of your level, repair the gun and leave you to go get it; you can keep the gun. "2 map transitions" means you get to go to Limansk from Army Warehouses, or to Wild Territory from Cordon, which is a short and relaxed walk to get an almost new PM.

The guns scale just like [Neutralization](/tasks/gt-tasks/heavy) does; in fact, these same lists of guns are used in [Transaction](/tasks/gt-tasks/transaction).
